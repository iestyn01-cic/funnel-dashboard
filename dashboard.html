How To Read This

Walk the funnel top to bottom. Find the ONE metric that contracted compared to last week. That's your bottleneck. Fix that one thing and leave everything else alone.

Green dot = above benchmark. Yellow = close to benchmark. Red = below benchmark. Gray = no data yet.

Do not change multiple variables at once or you'll never know what moved the needle. Fix one metric, measure for a week, then move to the next.

How To Automate This Dashboard

Overview

This dashboard reads from dashboard_data.json. You automate it by setting up Zapier zaps that push data into that file. Three data sources feed it: Meta Ads, your CRM (GHL), and your sales team.

Zap 1: Meta Ads Data (Daily + Weekly + Monthly)

1
Trigger: Schedule by Zapier (Daily at 9 AM) Runs once every morning to pull yesterday's ad data
2
Action: Facebook Ads (Find Ad Account Insights) Pull spend, impressions, link clicks, CPM, CTR for the previous day
3
Action: Webhooks by Zapier (POST) Send the data to your dashboard webhook endpoint. The dashboard updates dashboard_data.json automatically.

Zap 2: GHL CRM Call Bookings (Real-Time)

1
Trigger: GoHighLevel (New Appointment Booked) Fires every time someone books a call through your funnel
2
Action: Webhooks by Zapier (POST) Push booking data (name, source, setter, timestamp) to the dashboard

Zap 3: GHL CRM Call Dispositions (Real-Time)

1
Trigger: GoHighLevel (Appointment Status Changed) Fires when a closer marks a call as showed, closed, or no-show
2
Action: Webhooks by Zapier (POST) Push disposition data (closer name, showed/no-show, closed/lost, deal value) to the dashboard

Zap 4: Payment / Revenue Collected (Real-Time)

1
Trigger: Stripe / Payment Processor (New Payment) Fires when a customer pays. If you use a different processor, swap this trigger.
2
Action: Webhooks by Zapier (POST) Push revenue data (amount, customer, closer attribution) to the dashboard

Zap 5: Slack Daily Summary (Optional but Recommended)

1
Trigger: Schedule by Zapier (Daily at 6 PM) End of day summary posted to your Slack channel
2
Action: Slack (Send Message) Posts daily spend, leads, calls booked, calls showed, calls closed, and cash collected to your team channel

What You Need To Do

1
Set up the 5 Zapier zaps above Each one takes about 10 minutes. Start with Zap 1 (Meta Ads) since that's your primary channel.
2
Configure GHL to track call dispositions Make sure your closers are marking every call as showed, no-show, closed, or lost in GHL. This data feeds the dashboard automatically.
3
Tag every lead with its traffic source in GHL Use unique funnel URLs per channel so GHL can tag leads by source. This gives you per-channel ROAS instead of blended guessing.
4
Give each closer a unique calendar embed Each closer gets their own Calendly/GHL booking link in the funnel. This traces every deal back to the closer who closed it.
